Neighbourhoods

Where people live in Squamish.

Squamish stretches about 15 km up the valley from Howe Sound. Each part of town has a different feel, commute and price point.

Downtown

Walkable streets, condos and townhomes, cafés and the estuary on your doorstep.

Walkable · Condos

Valleycliffe

A family neighbourhood beneath the Stawamus Chief, with trails and the Smoke Bluffs nearby.

Families · Trails

Before you buy

What to look into in Squamish.

Every purchase is different, and a local agent, inspector and lawyer are worth it. These are the Squamish-specific questions buyers most often ask.

  • Flood and hazard mappingParts of the valley sit near rivers and steep slopes. Ask your agent to check the municipal hazard maps for any property.
  • Strata documentsFor condos and townhomes, read the minutes, depreciation report and rules (rentals, pets, bikes, storage) before you commit.
  • Commute and the highwayMany residents commute on Highway 99. Drive it at rush hour, and in the rain, before you decide.
  • Storage for gearBikes, boards, skis: a lockable garage or storage room matters more here than in most towns.
  • Sun and viewsThe valley walls shade some streets in winter. Visit at different times of day.
Living here

Why people move to Squamish.

Climbing, mountain biking, lakes and the ocean all within a few minutes, a real downtown, and Vancouver about an hour away. For more on what to do here, see SquamishGuide.com and the Squamish field guides.
